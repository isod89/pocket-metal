POCKET METAL

A firmware for metal on the Donner Pocket X and the M-VAVE BlackBox: sixty sounds for every style, fitted high-gain amps, down-tuning, stereo.

v2 Donner Pocket X M-VAVE BlackBox Free and open source
60 SOUNDSEvery style, from NWOBHM and thrash to death, black, doom, drone, djent and thall, with cleans and leads, all at the same loudness.
FITTED AMPSTen high-gain amps and five pedals fitted to captures of the real gear, on two cores: the chain on one, the delay and the reverb on the other.
TUNED DOWNFrom E to F# standard or an octave down, to the cent, while the guitar stays in E. A gate made for palm-muted riffs.

This browser has no Web MIDI. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS).

1Connect the pedal

Turn the pedal on, plug it in with a USB data cable, and close any other app that uses it (M-EFCS, M-UPGRADE, a DAW). The browser asks for MIDI access: allow it.

2Install POCKET METAL

Version …. It replaces the firmware the pedal runs now (the official one, OpenPocket, or another one on OpenPocket's base). Your OpenPocket presets, NAM models and cabinet IRs stay on the pedal.

During the install (1 to 2 minutes), do not unplug the pedal or switch it off, and keep this tab open. The page checks the package (its SHA-256 and every CRC) before anything is sent, and the pedal checks it again before anything is written. If the install stops, do not switch the pedal off: plug the cable back in and press Resume. A BlackBox on M-VAVE's firmware V22 or later is not supported yet: the page and the pedal refuse it, and nothing is written.

This version of POCKET METAL has not run on a pedal yet. Install it on a pedal you can recover: keep your backup (SD card / fm1buddy) or the official firmware file at hand. Please report how it goes on GitHub.

3Go back

To OpenPocket

OpenPocket's installer puts OpenPocket back on any pedal that runs POCKET METAL: connect there and press Back to OpenPocket. Your OpenPocket presets are still on the pedal.

To the official firmware

Pick your pedal's official file (.fwsc), from Donner or M-VAVE. It is checked here, in your browser, and sent to no server.

Rescue and advanced options

If the pedal stays in update mode, reconnect it: the Resume button appears.

A pedal that this page cannot reach at all (a black screen, or restarts with no USB) is restored with OpenPocket's USB rescue tool: RESCUE.md. The button below puts a pedal that runs POCKET METAL into the chip's UBOOT mode, for that tool or the SD card.
