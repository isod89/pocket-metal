## POCKET METAL v2, the first release on its own

- Sixty sounds, at least one for each style of metal, from NWOBHM and thrash to death, black, doom, drone, sludge,
  djent, thall, metalcore, mathcore, crust, folk and Viking, with cleans and leads, all at the same loudness.
- Ten high-gain amps and five pedals fitted to captures of the real gear, beside OpenPocket's own.
- Two cores: the chain on the second, the delay and the reverb on the first, as OpenPocket runs its engine. The first
  build ran on one core and crackled on its heaviest sounds.
- A gate made for palm-muted riffs; down-tuning from E to F# standard or an octave down, to the cent; an octaver, a
  harmonizer, a whammy; stereo width with the low end in the middle.
- Black and blood screens, a tuner on both footswitches, MIDI.
- Sounds saved with the first build stay in their slots.

Not yet run on a pedal in this version: please report the `DSP` figure of the heaviest sounds, and any crackle, in the
issues.
